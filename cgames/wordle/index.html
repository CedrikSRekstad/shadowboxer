How to Play

Goal

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. Each guess must be a valid word.

Color Clues

A Correct letter, correct position
B Correct letter, wrong position
C Letter not in the word

Game Modes

Normal: Classic solo Wordle.

Custom: Player 1 sets a secret word for Player 2.

2P Race: Both players guess the same word side-by-side. P1 types normally, P2 holds Right Shift.

2P Shared: Players alternate turns on the same board.

2P Hints: One player gives text hints, the other guesses.

Scoring

Points by guesses: 1st=1000, 2nd=800, 3rd=600, 4th=400, 5th=250, 6th=100

Time bonus: under 30s=+200, under 60s=+100, under 120s=+50

Word length multiplier: 4=x0.8, 5=x1.0, 6=x1.2, 7=x1.5, 8=x2.0
