HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

One player is "IT" and must tag the other player. Don't be "IT" when the timer runs out!

KEYBOARD

Player 1: WASD to move, W to jump, E or Shift to dash

Player 2: Arrow keys to move, Up to jump, M to dash

MOVES

🏃 Dash: Quick burst of speed (cooldown)
🧗 Wall Jump: Slide on walls & jump off them
🦘 Double Jump: Jump again in mid-air!

MOBILE

Use the on-screen joystick, jump & dash buttons

POWER-UPS

⚡ Speed   ❄️ Freeze   🛡️ Shield   🧲 Magnet
🔀 Swap   🔮 Teleport   🍄 Shrink

WORLDS

🌲 Forest   🌋 Lava   ❄️ Ice   🍬 Candy   🚀 Space
🌊 Ocean   🏰 Castle   ☁️ Sky
